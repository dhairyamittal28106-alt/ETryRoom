Privacy

ETryRoom stores account data needed to provide saved fitting profiles, product size charts, uploaded garment images, and saved looks. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Sessions use an HttpOnly cookie.

Camera access is processed in your browser. Camera frames are not uploaded. Body measurements you enter are stored with your account so they can be used for size comparisons.

If you request a password reset, ETryRoom uses your email address to send a short-lived reset link through the service's configured email provider. Reset tokens are stored as hashes, expire after 30 minutes, and are pruned during recovery requests.

You can delete your account and its profiles, products, images, and saved looks from the account menu. The service operator must protect the database and configure HTTPS with secure cookies before exposing this server to the public internet.

When a seller publishes a size guide, its brand, product name, garment photo, source URL, and entered size measurements are visible in the public catalog. Public measurements and source links are entered by sellers and are not independently verified by ETryRoom. Account email, body measurements, private products, camera frames, and saved looks are not public.
